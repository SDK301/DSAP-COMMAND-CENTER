How to read this calendar, and what is not in it Click any day to set the whole dashboard to that night; if the day carries an event, the list below scrolls to it. Cells tinted blue are moonless nights (under 15% lit) \u2014 your new-moon windows. Phases, eclipses, oppositions, elongations and conjunctions are computed from ephemerides rather than read from a stored list, so any month you navigate to is calculated fresh. Meteor peaks follow the IMO working list and can shift by about a day between years. Comets are deliberately absent \u2014 predicting them needs current orbital elements, and comet brightness forecasts are unreliable even for professionals, so anything shown here would be invented rather than computed. Check the Minor Planet Center or COBS instead.

Your data, demo lock & sharing

Capture status, session logs, added targets and preferences are stored in this browser only — they are never uploaded anywhere. Clearing site data, switching browser or switching device will lose them, so export a backup before you move.

Use this to carry your logs from the Claude copy of this app to a hosted copy, or between machines.

Showing this to someone else
Two things worth knowing. First, your data never travels with the file — it lives in your browser, so anyone you send the HTML to opens a completely empty copy and cannot see or touch your sessions at all. The risk is only when someone uses your device. For that, the Lock button in the toolbar puts the app in read-only demo mode: status, sessions, notes and images are frozen, and a PIN is needed to unlock. Be clear-eyed about what that is though — this is a single HTML file, so anyone who opens browser developer tools can get past it. It keeps an audience from clicking things during a demo; it is not a safe.

If you want someone to see a populated version, send them a backup file to import rather than handing over your device — they then edit their own copy, and yours is untouched.

Demo lock & sharing

Set a PIN with the Lock button in the header first. Then use Prepare a copy for sharing below to get the line to paste into a copy you host or hand out, so it opens locked for everyone else.

Forgot your PIN? Reload the page with ?reset added to the end of the web address (e.g. yoursite.com/index.html?reset) — it clears the PIN so you can set a new one. You can never be permanently locked out of your own copy.

Dark-sky sites & safety notes

Sky-darkness gain is calculated against your home site: it is how much faster a darker sky reaches the same signal-to-noise, so a 4× figure means one hour there is worth four at home.

On solo trips, and on live location sharing
Worth being straight with you: no web app can share your location once the mobile network drops, because there is nothing to send it over. That needs a satellite device — a Garmin inReach or Zoleo with a scheduled tracking interval, or satellite SOS on a recent iPhone or Pixel. If you are shooting alone at Chakrata or Chopta, that is the tool for the job, not this page. What this app can sensibly do is help you pick sites where you are not isolated in the first place, which is why the notes column flags whether a site has lodging or people nearby.

I have estimated the Bortle ratings, distances and drive times — I have not measured or driven them. Treat them as a starting point and correct them from your own experience; given your work across Uttarakhand you will know several of these better than any published map.

Observing sites & travel guidance
Dehradun Valley (~30.3° N) — home base. Targets above Dec +20° are broadly workable from urban/suburban setups with dual-band filters cutting light pollution on emission targets and SNRs.

Short trips (<1.5 hrs) — George Everest Ridge / Mussoorie Heights (Bortle 4–5, clear southern horizon); Benog Wildlife Sanctuary Ridge (gets above the valley aerosol inversion).

Dark sky expeditions (>3 hrs) — Chopta / Deoriatal (Bortle 2–3); Chakrata / Koti Kanasar (Bortle 3). Reserve these for integrated flux nebulae, dust complexes and ultra-faint SNR shells.

Southern horizon limits. From Dehradun anything below about Dec −40° never clears 20° altitude. Omega Centauri peaks near 12°, Puppis A near 17° — both need a genuinely flat southern horizon, so plan them from a ridge site.
